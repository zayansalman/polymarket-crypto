## 1. What the 5-minute program has established (all venue-true)

1. **No directional edge exists at our latency class.** The unfiltered control (v0) sits at
   ~$0 over 400+ settled shadow trades. Every filtered variant that led the race (v7, then
   v8) regressed to null as n grew; v8's PnL was one leg (Up) and one day. Edge archaeology:
   0/75 slices after FDR. Regime attribution: 0/12 cells, permutation p=0.21–0.65.
2. **Fees were the entire live loss.** Lifetime real money −$19.35 over 351 fills; June era
   gross +$6.27 vs taker fees −$23.51. Realized win rate ~55% vs fee breakeven 55.7% at our
   average price — a coin flip paying a 2.6%-of-turnover rake.
3. **The counterparties are subsidized professionals.** Polymarket pays makers a 20% rebate
   of the taker fees they fill plus a Liquidity Rewards pool (>$5M/month, Apr 2026). Makers
   pay zero fees. Our #130 backtest measured their adverse selection from the other side
   (resting quotes fill 98% of losers, 75% of winners); our #137 telemetry shows 77% of our
   entries crossed at placement (paid the rake). The profitable actors in this market are
   execution businesses — subsidized MMs, last-second latency snipers, near-resolution
   scalpers — not predictors. Nobody wins by calling 5-minute BTC direction.
4. **One question remains open**: `cushion_fresh_v7_f45` (replay OOS CI [+0.275, +0.887],
   fee-true) started accruing live-race data 2026-07-10 11:11 UTC. Its deploy bar is ~56
   settled trades (~1–2 weeks). This is the program's last live hypothesis.

## 2. Options (MECE)

**A. Run the race to its pre-registered verdict.** Cost ≈ zero (loop runs; the 6h agent loop
reports). Value: f45 either replicates (first legitimate deploy signal this project has ever
had) or completes the null cleanly. Sunset date if nothing clears: **2026-08-27**.

**B. Compete in 5m microstructure (become the maker / the sniper).** Requires sub-100ms

incumbents. Prize at our feasible size: ~$2–4/day. **Rejected** — wrong game for this

**C. Repoint the lab at slow, low/zero-fee categories.** Polymarket fee schedule (V2,
2026-03-30): crypto takers pay 0.07 — the *highest* rake; finance/politics 0.04;
**geopolitics/world events are fee-free**. In slow markets the edge dimension flips from
latency (we lose) to forecasting skill + calibration (untested for us, and the only game a
solo analytical operator can plausibly win). What transfers from this repo: fee-true
ledger discipline, the shadow/paper race framework, pre-registration + FDR habits, risk
gates, the replay-harness pattern. Honest caveats: books are thinner, capital locks up for
weeks, competition includes sharp forecasters, and *our forecasting skill is an untested
hypothesis* — it gets a paper test before a dollar moves.

**D. Leave prediction markets.** Legitimate, out of this repo's scope.

## 4. Pre-registered pilot — slow-market forecasting skill (no capital)

*Hypothesis*: the operator+model can produce probability forecasts on slow Polymarket
markets that beat the market-implied probability after spread, in categories where fees
are 0–4%.

- **Universe**: markets resolving ≤60 days out, in fee-free or ≤0.04 categories, with
  top-of-book depth ≥ $500 and spread ≤ 3c. Target n ≥ 30 resolutions.
- **Protocol**: forecast is logged (journal table, timestamped) BEFORE looking at the
  market's current price — anchoring is the known failure mode. Then the executable quote
  is snapshotted alongside.
- **Scoring**: Brier score vs the market-implied baseline (skill = market Brier − ours);
  fee-true simulated PnL at the snapshotted executable quotes, only on forecasts diverging
  ≥5c from market.
- **Success bar (pre-registered)**: positive Brier skill AND simulated PnL 95% CI > 0 over
  ≥30 resolutions. Anything less = no demonstrated skill = do not fund.
- **Non-goals**: no automation of forecast generation in v1, no live orders, no crypto
  categories, no changes to the running 5m race.
