The short answer

    Hold or scalp

    Holding means keeping the shares until the window settles at $1 or $0. Scalping sells early at the bid when a trade is up 10%, down 8%, near the end of the window, or when the bot's edge disappears. Each sale pays the spread and a second fee.

    How much to trade

    Size doesn't change the edge per share, only how big the swings are. "Worst losing streak" is the biggest drop from a high point. Large orders can't always fill, because only so many shares are offered at the asking price.
