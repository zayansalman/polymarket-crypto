## 1. Practical notes that affect every candidate

**Getting P(up) out of each model type**
- Quantile heads (Chronos-Bolt/-2, TimesFM 2.5/3.0, TiRex, Moirai 2, TTM r3, Toto 2.0, PatchTST-FM, EXAONE, Timer-S1): interpolate the forecast CDF of the next close (or 5pm price) at the prior 5pm close; P(up) = 1 − F(prior close). With only 9 levels (0.1…0.9) the tails are coarse; Chronos-2/EXAONE (21) and PatchTST-FM-r2 (99) are finer.
- Sample-based (Chronos T5, Sundial, Kronos, Lag-Llama, Moirai 1.x, Toto 1.0): P(up) = share of sampled paths ending above the prior close; use ≥100 samples. Kronos's recommended temperature T=0.6 sharpens distributions → recalibrate.
- Evidence says median forecasts of returns collapse toward zero (Wan et al. 2026) and TSFM 80% intervals covered only 65–76% on post-cutoff FX (2609.10357) → read the whole distribution, then recalibrate P(up) walk-forward (isotonic/Platt) and always compare against always-up and last-day-sign baselines (Cheung 2026 showed an "80% DA" was base rate).

**Intraday-to-5pm framing**: feed the hourly CL series up to the current hour and forecast the remaining hours to 17:00 ET (Kronos, Chronos-2, TimesFM, TiRex, FlowState all accept arbitrary horizons; FlowState is explicitly timescale-invariant). Daily framing: 1-step-ahead on 5pm-sampled closes.

**Look-ahead rules**
- Test points must postdate every compared model's pretraining data *and* not overlap correlated series (Meyer et al.: a model that saw other indices beat a clean model by ~12% MAE on an unseen index).
- Clean windows: Kronos ≥ 2024-07; TimesFM 2.5/3.0 corpora ≤ Nov 2023 (real series) + synthetic; Chronos-2 / TiRex / Moirai 2 / PatchTST-FM / Sundial use public corpora that end ≤ ~2023 and contain little finance (Kronos paper estimates financial share: Sundial 1.02%, MOMENT 1.60%, Chronos 0.45%, Moirai 0.10%, Timer 0.03%, Lag-Llama 0.01%, TimesFM/Time-MoE < 0.01%); EXAONE and `chronos-2-synth` are synthetic-only; FinText provides per-year vintages.
- For pre-2024 backtests, prefer FinText vintages, EXAONE, `chronos-2-synth`, or from-scratch group-A models.

**8 GB M2 feasibility (inference)**: comfortable ≤ ~400M params fp32 (≈1.6 GB). Not feasible: Timer-S1 (8.3B MoE, ≥40 GB VRAM recommended), Toto-2.0-2.5B (9.1 GB), Toto-2.0-1B borderline (3.9 GB), FinCast (3.97 GB pickle) marginal. Reverso requires CUDA FlashFFTConv (no CPU path documented). Apple-silicon ports: `kronos-mlx`, TimesFM 3.0 MLX backend, TiRex-mlx and FlowState-mlx community ports.

### B-detail. Kronos (verified against arXiv 2508.02739 v1 full text + GitHub README, 2026-09-14)
- Links: [github shiyu-coder/Kronos](https://github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.02739) (AAAI 2026) · HF [Kronos-mini](https://huggingface.co/NeoQuasar/Kronos-mini) / [-small](https://huggingface.co/NeoQuasar/Kronos-small) / [-base](https://huggingface.co/NeoQuasar/Kronos-base) · MLX port [gxcsoccer/kronos-mlx-*](https://huggingface.co/gxcsoccer/kronos-mlx-small)
- Sizes: mini 4.1M (Tokenizer-2k, ctx 2048) · small 24.7M (ctx 512; safetensors 99 MB) · base 102.3M (ctx 512) · large 499.2M **not released**. MIT license.
- Architecture: BSQ tokenizer turns each OHLCVA bar into 2 hierarchical sub-tokens; decoder-only AR transformer; temporal embeddings (minute/hour/day-of-week/day-of-month/month). Per-window z-score + clip ±5 (instance normalization, causal).
- Pretraining corpus: ">12 billion K-line records", 45 exchanges / 30+ countries, 7 frequencies 1-min→weekly; equities dominant; **"we increase the sampling weights for data from crypto, futures, and forex markets"** → futures (very likely incl. NYMEX CL) are in the corpus.
- **Data cutoff: "The pre-training data for Kronos extends up to June 2024. Consequently, our test period for all tasks begins in July 2024."** → any WTI backtest before 2024-07 is contaminated; clean OOS window = 2024-07 → today (~26 months, ~540 trading days).
- Output: sampled paths (temperature T, top_p, sample_count). Paper uses T=0.6, top_p=0.9, N=10 for return forecasting. P(up) = fraction of sampled paths with close_{t+1} > close_t (needs larger N, e.g. 50–200, for a calibrated probability; note T<1 sharpens and will distort calibration).
- Covariates: none beyond OHLCV(A) + timestamps. Volume/amount optional (randomly zeroed 5% in training; crypto/FX evaluated price-only).
- Fine-tuning: official scripts released 2025-08-17 (Qlib A-share pipeline + `finetune_csv`).
- Paper evaluation (what was actually tested): test from July 2024 on stocks (9 exchanges), Binance crypto spot, >1,000 FX pairs — **no futures/commodities in the test set**. Metrics: IC/RankIC of predicted price *series* (level-like, red flag), IC/RankIC of H-step return (more relevant), realized-vol MAE/R², and a CSI300/CSI800 top-k long-only backtest (H=10 days, 0.15% costs): Kronos-base AER 0.189, IR 1.52 averaged; Chronos-large AER −0.030; Moirai-large 0.158/1.25; TimesFM 0.107/1.19; PatchTST 0.146/1.25. Daily lookback 40 / horizon 12 in forecasting tasks; 1h lookback 80 / horizon 12. No directional-accuracy metric reported; no naive/random-walk baseline in the IC tables.
- CPU on 8 GB M2: mini/small trivial; base fine (~400 MB fp32). Cost is AR sampling: 1-step daily forecast × N paths is cheap; hourly bars to 5pm (≤ 8–24 steps) × N=100 paths is seconds–minutes per day on CPU; MLX port helps.
- Community evidence (HF cards): [zubziretta/Kronos-small-BIST](https://huggingface.co/zubziretta/Kronos-small-BIST) 10-bar direction on 192 held-out windows: base 1d 52.1% (CI 45.0–59.0), fine-tuned 1d 55.2% (CI 48.1–62.1), MAPE never beats naive; [loginil/Kronos-small-btc-15m-dir-pathe](https://huggingface.co/loginil/Kronos-small-btc-15m-dir-pathe) frozen-backbone direction head, BTC 15m val acc 50.80%.

## A. Group A — trained-from-scratch / fine-tunable architectures with oil or commodity-futures use

Bottom line: there are **no public pretrained weights for any oil-specific deep model** worth using (HF has only hobby Spaces and one WTI anomaly autoencoder). The credible evidence is (i) Oxford-Man style Sharpe-trained sequence models on daily futures panels that include crude, and (ii) a handful of leakage-aware student/research repos on WTI next-day direction that report roughly 50–55% accuracy. Most oil deep-learning papers forecast price **levels** with decomposition hybrids and report R²/MAPE — listed in A3 as red-flag examples.

## E. Published evidence: pretrained / deep sequence models on returns, direction, commodities, oil

Bottom line: no rigorous OOS study of any TSFM (Chronos, TimesFM, Moirai, TiRex, Toto, Sundial, Kronos, FinCast, EXAONE…) on **WTI/Brent daily direction** was found. Closest: (i) Oxford-Man DL benchmark with WTI/Brent in a daily futures universe (position/direction, gross), (ii) a CME-futures end-to-end study where DL lost to 1/N on the energy sleeve, (iii) a TSFM realized-volatility study that includes CL. On equities, zero-shot TSFMs sit at ~49–51% daily directional accuracy with negative OOS R².
